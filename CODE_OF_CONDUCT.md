## 1. Our Pledge

We as members and contributors of **VDE** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community around [VDE](https://github.com/VicRodger27/VDE).

## 2. Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other contributors and users;
- **Being respectful** of differing technical opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- **Giving and gracefully accepting** constructive code review and architectural feedback;
- **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- **Focusing on what is best** for the overall **VDE** community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks in issues, commits, or pull requests;
- Public or private harassment of any community member;
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission ("doxxing");
- Deliberate disruption of technical discussions, issue triage, or release workflows;
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative open-source setting.

## 3. Scope & Spaces

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including **GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and Community Discord**, and also applies when an individual is officially representing **VDE** or its community in public spaces.

## 4. Enforcement Guidelines & Violation Reporting

### How to Report Violations

If you experience, witness, or become aware of unacceptable behavior, please report it immediately:

- **Primary Contact Person:** Victor Kostin
- **Reporting Channels:** Confidential Email, Private Security Advisory, Anonymous Web Form, Direct Message to Primary Contact
- **Contact Details:** Refer to [CONTACT.md](CONTACT.md) for current private communication channels.
- **Initial Response SLA:** Within **48 hours**

When filing a report, please include as much of the following information as possible to expedite a fair review:
- Date, time, and location/channel of the incident
- Account(s) or individual(s) involved
- Detailed description of what occurred
- Screenshots, links, or verifiable evidence

### Who Handles Reports & Governance

All reports are investigated by **Victor Kostin**. Every reviewer is committed to confidentiality, neutrality, and the safety of the reporting party.

- **Conflict of Interest & Recusal:** If a report involves or names any member of the review body, that individual is immediately recused from receiving report details, investigating, or participating in resolution decisions.

- **Anti-Retaliation Protection:** **VDE** strictly prohibits retaliation of any kind against anyone who reports a violation in good faith or participates in an investigation.

### Potential Consequences & Enforcement Ladder

The review team will follow these guidelines when determining appropriate, proportional consequences:

#### Level 1: Correction
- **Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.
- **Consequence:** A private, written notice providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public or private apology may be requested.

#### Level 2: Formal Warning
- **Community Impact:** A violation through a single serious incident or a series of actions.
- **Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. Interaction restrictions with affected community members, including a mandatory cooling-off period from discussions.

#### Level 3: Temporary Suspension (30 days)
- **Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, harassment, or sustained inappropriate behavior.
- **Consequence:** A temporary ban (30 days) from any sort of interaction or public communication within project spaces, issue triage, and repositories.

#### Level 4: Permanent Ban
- **Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation, severe harassment, doxxing, or aggression toward individuals or classes of individuals.
- **Consequence:** A permanent ban from all project spaces, discussion forums, and community organization repositories.

### Appeals Process

Decisions may be appealed within 14 days of notice by submitting a written appeal. Appeals are reviewed by a neutral community lead not involved in the original complaint.
