# What this is

This is where the SVG icons are stored. Because I am making this with different LLM/VLMs, I am making my coding agent (Antigravity: Gemini 3.8 Flash Medium) have a choice of which SVG icons to choose from. The odd numbers are generated by DSeek (with DeepThink and maxed out settings). The even numbers are generated by GLM 5.3 Flash with Effort Max.
